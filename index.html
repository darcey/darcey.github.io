Darcey Masters (née Riley)

About me:

Wife of Luca Masters, mother of one child so far. I have two Etsy shops, where I sell beads and jewelry respectively.

In a past life, I studied natural language processing (NLP), first under Jason Eisner at Johns Hopkins University, and later under David Chiang at University of Notre Dame. My research focused on probabilistic modeling, with a particular interest on Bayesian epistemology; later I studied generation from language models. I left the field due to some combination of health issues, parental responsibilities, and a growing concern that AI may post an existential threat to humanity.
